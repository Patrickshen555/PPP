🔥 2026 Q1-Q2 Key Market Events

🔵 Jan–Feb 2026 — Pre-CNY Rush

CNY Shipping Peak + Ongoing Red Sea Disruption

  • Pre-CNY export rush combined with Red Sea diversion pushed Asia-Europe rates higher
  • CMA CGM introduced Peak Season Surcharge (PSS); mainstream strategy remained full Cape routing
  • SOLAS new regulation effective: mandatory reporting of lost containers at sea (container loss surged 181% in 2024)
🟠 Feb–Mar 2026 — Houthi Attacks Resume

Maersk's Two Failed Suez Resumption Attempts + Hormuz Escalation

  • Houthi forces resumed systematic attacks on commercial vessels in early February
  • Maersk ME11 service attempted Suez restoration on Mar 5 and Mar 12; both reversed within days
  • Iran-U.S. Hormuz conflict escalation → created dual chokepoint crisis: Red Sea + Hormuz simultaneously blocked
  • ~130 container vessels trapped inside Persian Gulf or anchored off Gulf of Oman
🔴 April 2026 — Stalemate

Bunker Price Surge + War Risk Premium Dispute + Rates at Elevated Volatility

  • VLSFO fuel spiked from $430/mt to peak $960/mt (+123%)
  • Forwarders criticized carriers' war risk surcharges as "excessive and non-standardized," some exceeding base freight cost itself
  • CMA CGM officially restored partial Suez services (FAL1/FAL3/INDAMEX)
  • Far East→USWC rates surged ~40% vs late Feb; Far East→N. Europe up ~20%
  • Industry-wide schedule reliability declined; most Top 10 carriers saw MoM deterioration

📈 Freight Rate Assessment:
① Short-term (1–3 months): Cost-driven increases will persist — Bunker Adjustment Factor (BAF) and War Risk Surcharge (WRS) dominate, not demand-driven. If VLSFO retreats from highs, May may see partial surcharge pullback.
② Scarcity value of Suez direct service is now clear: — With 90% of Far East-Med cargo forced around Cape of Good Hope, AEM's stable Suez direct capability represents a significant competitive edge (10–14 day time advantage).
③ Hormuz factor is a new variable: — If the strait remains closed, the capacity shortfall from ~130 trapped Gulf vessels will transmit globally, further lifting floor rates across all trade lanes.
④ Contract season (2026–27) supports elevated rate levels: — Carriers have strong incentive to maintain current levels entering annual contract negotiations; blank sailings expected at ~6% to manage capacity.
