🔄 Our Operating Model & Alternative Solutions

🟢 Primary Mode (Our Standard Operation): Vessels Sail Direct To KLF (Khor Fakkan) Or SOH (Sohar) For Berthing And Discharge → Truck/Rail Inland Transport To Each ME Destination (Dubai, Riyadh, Dammam, Doha, Abu Dhabi Etc.). KLF/SOH Are Located Outside The Gulf Of Hormuz/Strait Area — No Need To Cross The Blockade.

⚠️ Inland Transport Cost: Additional 5-8 Days Land Transit Time + $300-500/TEU Inland Freight. Main Corridors: SOH→Oman Inland Road→UAE; KLF→UAE Domestic Distribution.

Option B - JED Red Sea Line Transshipment: Some Cargo Can Route Via JED (Jeddah) Red Sea Line → Saudi Domestic Land Transport → Gulf States. Suitable For Cargo Not Extremely Time-Sensitive; Serves As A Supplementary Path To KLF/SOH.

Option C - Sea-Air Intermodal: High-Value Urgent Shipments Air-Freighted To DXB For Redistribution — Extremely High Cost But Controllable Transit Time. Recommended Only For Electronic Components/Emergency Spare Parts.
